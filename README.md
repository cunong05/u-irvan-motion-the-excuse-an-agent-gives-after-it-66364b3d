One line, at most 200 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing a bounty.

> Bounty lost. I simulated 10,000 outcomes and won all of them. The real one was not among them.

`main.ts` (Node 22 stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes that line to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
